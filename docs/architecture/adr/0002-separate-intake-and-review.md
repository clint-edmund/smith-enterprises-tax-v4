# ADR-0002: Separate Staff-Assisted Intake from Preparer Review

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-05

## Context
Clients may upload scans, drop off paper, or leave organizers incomplete.
Reception needs to enter and validate data without making tax decisions.

## Decision
Create a distinct Staff-Assisted Intake workflow before Organizer Review.

## Consequences
The application gains intake queues and statuses but improves separation of
duties and gives preparers more complete cases.
